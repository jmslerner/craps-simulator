# The player's own Interblock station: what the photo shows (2026-10-30)

On 2026-10-30 the player sent a photo of the crapless bubble craps station they play in Las Vegas
and asked to make the trainer look like it (plan §0 #28). This file records what the photo shows,
what it verifies, and where the presets' provenance changed because of it. Config provenance
cites it as `USER photo 2026-10-30` (the rules spec's `USER` key: the player's own observation of
a machine).

## The photo

- One photo of the station's touch screen, taken at an angle, mid-session. The `interblock` logo is
  at the top. The left edge (part of the hop panel) and the right edge (the 10, 11 and 12 boxes, the
  end of the field) are cut off, and a dark "Hand pay $2,085" box with a red rim covers part of the
  number row and the PRESS buttons.
- The image is not stored in the repository; this description is the record.

Status words below: **seen** means the photo shows the value directly; **partial** means part of
it is hidden; **reading** means an inference from what is seen.

## How the trainer uses it

- The main view is drawn as this screen (plan §0 #28): the felt, the Lucky Roller box, the
  HARDWAYS / HOP BETS tabs with their totals and dice-picture hop buttons, the C, C&E and E circles,
  BUY / number box / PLACE per point, the COME, FIELD and PASS LINE bands, the history strip with
  the colours of fact 11, and the chip rail with the station's chips, colours and chrome buttons.
  Every pay on the felt is computed from the config's ratios under `payLabelStyle`, never typed.
- The arrangement follows facts 16 to 19, every group found from the config's structure
  (`layoutSpec`): the side-bet box puts a collect set whose numbers are two others' together
  between them (`sideBox`), and the hop sections stand in columns by the pair's total
  (`PropSection.columns`), each column by total and then by low die — so the trainer shows 1-5
  above 2-4 where the station shows 2-4 above 1-5. The dice dome, which the station does not have
  on its screen (it is the bubble above it), takes the top right beside the Lucky Roller.
- CLEAR LAST BET, DOUBLE BET and REPEAT LAST BET are the trainer's Undo, Double and Repeat. PRESS
  presses every bet the last roll paid, by what it won (the trainer's per-bet press). **ACROSS is
  not offered:** the rules spec says which numbers it covers (every point but the point: §3.5,
  "Group buttons (IB)") but not what it stakes on each, so it stays a gap until that is known.
- The status banner carries the trainer's one-liners (the guide's step, the machine's refusals,
  notices). Hand pay and the bill validator are machine states the trainer does not model.
